# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, review evidence in good
faith, and separate technical disagreement from personal criticism.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- explaining assumptions and verification limits;
- providing reproducible evidence;
- welcoming corrections;
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds;
- prioritizing operator and bystander safety in robotics discussions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publication of
private information, and unsafe pressure to test on uncontrolled hardware are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `yujinhong3@gmail.com`. Reports will be
reviewed with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.
Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict
participation when necessary.
